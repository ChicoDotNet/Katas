# Licensing per independent kata

The root MIT LICENSE covers only original files whose rights are owned by their contributors or were licensed compatibly. No root declaration overrides upstream rights. Keep SPDX identifiers and LICENSE/NOTICE evidence for every imported independent asset.

1. Original solutions, tests, orchestration and Ninja: MIT by default.
2. Public-domain/CC0 descriptions: verified source link and attribution when possible.
3. Third-party GPL-3.0 code: use only under its actual terms, ideally in a self-contained independent kata; do not simply label its files MIT. Combined derivative programs may require GPL terms.
4. CC BY/SA texts and code: check original terms and compatibility before remixing.
5. Unlicensed or restricted problem statements/tests: **link only**, do not republish.

Do not use a GPL license for the entire repo as a substitute for reviewing rights of individual contents.

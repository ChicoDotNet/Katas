# OBS-018 — Deterministic coordinate conventions in spatial searching

One graph or grid can admit multiple correct witnesses. For repeatable tests, pin the coordinate origin, direction order, boundary policy and tie-break, then test these decisions explicitly. A generated target word may incidentally exist at a different, earlier location: compare with a full independent reference search, not simply the injected position. The handwritten reverse-diagonal fixture originally misspelled a path and was corrected before acceptance. Local observation, not measured general Ninja transfer speed.
